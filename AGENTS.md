## What This Project Is

**BROADSIDE HYPERION** is a browser-based, real-time multiplayer LAN party starship tactical arena combat game. Two teams crew rival starships operated by a 3-role bridge crew (Helm, Weapons, Engineer) across Desktop PCs (Mouse + Keyboard) and Tablets/iPads (Multi-Touch), with a cinematic Spectator View (`/spectate`) for a central TV/projector. Powered by an authoritative 60Hz Node.js + WebSocket physics and simulation server.

## Key Gotchas & Decisions

### 1. True Newtonian Physics & Thruster Torque ($\tau = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$)
Ships have zero friction in vacuum. Each of the 6 RCS thruster banks (`bowPort`, `bowStarboard`, `sternPort`, `sternStarboard`, `dorsalRoll`, `ventralRoll`) and the Main Drive apply force at their physical mounting coordinates relative to the Center of Mass. Destroying or unpowering one thruster bank (e.g., `bowPort`) causes strafing maneuvers to fire only the remaining bank (`sternPort`), imparting asymmetric torque that spins the ship.

### 2. Finite Resource Pool & Dynamic Sharing on the 10x10 Grid
The Engineer's grid is strictly a **10x10 rectangular matrix (100 tiles)** with Reactor (`(2,3)`) and Coolant (`(2,6)`) supplying **11 external perimeter systems** via rotatable conduits matching the diagram layout (see Gotcha #20). Each resource uses an `8.0`-unit baseline pool (`BASE_RESOURCE_POOL = 8.0`) so connecting all 11 systems allocates `8/11 ≈ 72.7%` (`~0.727`) per system (`10.0s` hyperspace charge needs `100%` power by disconnecting 3 systems or surging/boosting), while disconnecting non-essential systems concentrates up to `100%` (`1.0`) capacity onto surviving systems.

### 3. Repair Drones Depend on Reactor Online Status & 1.5%/s Subsystem Repair Rate
Repair Drones can fix both broken/burning 10x10 grid tiles AND damaged ship subsystems (RCS banks, main drive, turrets, stabilizers, hyperspace, and all 4 shield quadrants: `shields_fore`, `shields_aft`, `shields_port`, `shields_starboard`), docking internally at the Reactor `(2,3)` and stalling if the Reactor is offline/EMP'd. Subsystem repair rate is standardized at `SUBSYSTEM_REPAIR_RATE = 1.5`% per second across both standard and turbo drone modules while actively repairing on site.

### 4. Ballistic Velocity Inheritance & Spherical Railgun Projectiles
Kinetic and ordnance projectiles (Heavy Railgun spherical slugs, Homing Torpedoes) inherit the firing ship's instantaneous velocity vector ($\vec{v}_{\text{proj}} = \vec{v}_{\text{ship}} + \vec{v}_{\text{muzzle}}$). Twin Pulse Lasers (`fireLaser`) do **not** inherit the ship's motion ($\vec{v}_{\text{laser}} = \vec{v}_{\text{muzzle}}$), firing directly along the aimed 3D vector at `laserMuzzleSpeed` (`600` m/s). Heavy Railgun projectiles render as a spherical core + outer plasma halo sphere (`THREE.SphereGeometry`) in 3D (`syncProjectiles3D`) and a centered circle (`ctx.arc`) in the 2D canvas fallback (`drawProjectile`).

### 5. Authoritative 60Hz Loop with 5x Killing Blow Time Dilation
The server executes an authoritative 60Hz tick loop (`dt = 1/60`s). When either capital ship hull reaches 0 HP, the simulation step dilates to $simDt = dt \times 0.2$ (5x slow motion) for 3.0 seconds (180 ticks), broadcasting cinematic kill-cam events to bridge stations and the spectator screen.

### 6. AI Auto-Crew Tactical Doctrine, Newtonian Intercept & Combat Stances
Unmanned stations on Alpha or Omega are seamlessly operated by AI bots coordinated by `evaluateTacticalSituation(ship, enemyShip, roomState)`:
- **Shared Tactical Stances**:
  - `'brawler'` (default offensive, 500–900m): closes distance and angles strongest shields.
  - `'flank_weak_quad'` (550–880m): circles toward the enemy's depleted shield quadrant (`weakestEnemyQuadHp < 50` while `strongest > 90`).
  - `'railgun_sniper'` (1250–1650m): active when lasers are overheated/damaged or when configured via `aiPreferredStance`, kiting outside 1200m laser range while sniping with the 2200m Railgun.
  - `'evasive_regen'` (1150–1550m): disengages outside enemy laser range to recharge when all shields are `< 20%` or fore shield is broken and hull is `< 60%`.
- **Newtonian $\Delta\vec{v}$ Intercept & Orbital Anti-Drift**: `AIHelm` computes desired intercept velocity vector $\vec{v}_{\text{des}}$ and steers along velocity error $\Delta\vec{v} = \vec{v}_{\text{des}} - \vec{v}_{\text{ship}}$, actively cancelling tangential orbital velocity $\vec{v}_{\text{tan}}$ and only burning main drive when aligned with $\Delta\vec{v}$ (`alignCos > 0.5`). Angular PD rate damping (`-0.45 * omega`) prevents yaw/pitch overshoot, and lateral strafe is gated until heading errors are small (`< 0.35 rad`) to preserve 4.5x `rcsTurnAssist`.
- **Proximity Asteroid Shatter & Pinpoint Gunnery**: `AIWeapons` evaluates `findTacticalShatterAsteroid` to target intact asteroids whose dynamic debris blast will damage an enemy hull (`<= 240m` from asteroid surface) while keeping the shooter safe (`>= 260m`). Pinpoint railgun targeting selects high-value targets via `selectOptimalSubsystemTarget` (`hyperspace` charging, `weapons_railgun`, `mainDrive`, `weapons_laser`).
- **Tactical Dynamic Engineering Boost**: `AIEngineer` automatically coordinates up to 3 Phase 13 subsystem boosts based on tactical stance (`railgun_sniper` boosts railgun, main drive, and RCS; `evasive_regen` boosts facing shield, RCS, and main drive; `brawler` boosts lasers, railgun, and torpedo/shield), sorting drone repair queues by root conduit proximity and subsystem criticality.
- **Flak & Tactical Roll**: `AIWeapons` detonates Point-Defense Flak automatically when incoming torpedoes or armed tether mines come within 190m in 3D. `AIHelm` fires physical roll RCS (`roll = 1.0` / `-1.0`, followed by `stopRoll(true)`) whenever the currently facing lateral shield quadrant (`port` or `starboard`) drops below 25% while the opposite quadrant is > 60% and powered.

### 7. Dual Format Gravity Well Position Support
Gravity wells in procedural sectors provide position data via `well.pos.x / well.pos.y`, while raw physics tests supply `well.x / well.y`. The gravity calculation in `src/physics/gravity.js` and `GameRoom` normalizes both shapes so inverse-square acceleration works consistently across all subsystems.

### 8. 60Hz Event Buffering Across 30Hz Snapshots
The physics and combat simulation runs at 60Hz, while state snapshots are broadcast at 30Hz (every 2 ticks). Instantaneous one-shot events (`laser_fired`, `railgun_hit`, `torpedo_hit`, `collision`, `killing_blow`, etc.) emitted either in `step()` or via `handleClientMessage()` are pushed into `this.recentEventsBuffer` in addition to `this.events`. When `getSnapshot()` is called, it returns all buffered events from the intervening simulation ticks and clears the buffer, preventing one-shot FX from being dropped between network broadcasts.

### 9. SpaceRenderer Options Object & Lead Target Unification
`SpaceRenderer.prototype.render(snapshot, options)` expects an options object `{ cameraPos, zoom, myShipTeam, showLeadReticle, leadReticle }`. For robustness, if a raw ship entity is passed as the second argument (`options.pos && !options.cameraPos`), the renderer normalizes it gracefully. When rendering intercept reticles, the renderer checks `const lead = options.leadReticle || myShip.leadTarget;`, allowing both client-calculated predictions and authoritative server-calculated targeting to be rendered seamlessly.

### 10. Authoritative Server-Calculated `ship.leadTarget`
In addition to client-side targeting in `controls-weapons.js`, the authoritative 60Hz simulation in `GameRoom.prototype.step()` calculates `calculateLeadTarget(shooterPos, shooterVel, targetPos, targetVel, 600)` every tick for active capital ships and serializes `leadTarget` into ship snapshots. When an enemy ship is destroyed, `leadTarget` drops to `null`. This ensures AI crew bots and spectators always have immediate access to authoritative intercept vectors without client-side calculation dependencies.

### 11. SAS Pilot Decouple and Toggle Semantics
`RigidBody.prototype.toggleSas(active)` accepts an explicit boolean state or toggles `!this.sasActive` when called without arguments. The client `HelmController` sends `{ type: 'helm_input', toggleSas: true }` on toggle actions and `{ sasActive: boolean }` for explicit states. `GameRoom` evaluates `msg.sasActive !== undefined ? ship.body.toggleSas(Boolean(msg.sasActive)) : (msg.toggleSas === true ? ship.body.toggleSas(!ship.body.sasActive) : ...)` to ensure keyboard (`Z`) and HUD toggle button presses invert SAS attitude stabilization rather than forcing it continuously active.

### 12. True 3D Three.js WebGL Rendering & Offline LAN Vendor Bundle
`public/vendor/three.module.js` and `public/vendor/three.core.js` are bundled directly inside `public/vendor/` (as well as mounted at `/vendor` in `src/server/app.js`) so `/vendor/three.module.js` resolves offline over LAN via a `<script type="importmap">` in `public/index.html` and `public/spectate.html`. `SpaceRenderer` (`public/js/renderer.js`) maintains an authoritative `THREE.Scene` with a pitched 3D `PerspectiveCamera`, multi-deck 3D starship meshes (`hullRollGroup.rotation.x = ship.rollAngle`), 3D geodesic shield domes, and 3D tumbling icosahedron asteroids. To prevent HTML5 Canvas 2D context locking in browsers, `SpaceRenderer` initializes `THREE.WebGLRenderer` first when `WebGLRenderingContext` is present, falling back to `canvas.getContext('2d')` only in headless test environments.

### 13. Pure Battle-Focused `/spectate` TV Broadcast View
`/spectate` focuses 100% on the 3D battle arena with no top header bar and no ship detail sidebars. It displays only a compact floating corner status pill (`#spectate-match-status`), a start/restart match button (`#btn-spectate-start`, hidden during active combat and shown in lobby or when a match is over), and a center-screen flashing victor overlay (`#spectate-victor-overlay`) when a winner is decided.

### 14. Physical Roll RCS Kinematics (`dorsalRoll` / `ventralRoll`) & Automatic Orientation Modes
Instead of a canned 180° roll timer, longitudinal roll is a continuous 1D Newtonian rotation (`rollAngle`, `rollOmega`, `rollInertia`) with zero vacuum drag. Firing `dorsalRoll` ($+\tau_{\text{roll}}$) or `ventralRoll` ($-\tau_{\text{roll}}$) accelerates `rollOmega`; `stopRoll(true)` fires the opposing healthy roll RCS jet to brake `rollOmega -> 0` (failing if that opposing roll thruster is destroyed or unpowered). Derived `rollState` (`Math.cos(rollAngle) < 0 ? 1 : 0`) automatically swaps Port and Starboard shield quadrants in world space whenever the ship rolls past $\pm 90^\circ$. In addition, `RigidBody` provides 4 automatic yaw orientation flight computer modes (`prograde`, `retrograde`, `target`, `anti_target`) using a phase-plane braking-curve PD controller; manual turn input immediately cancels auto-orientation, and Ion Nebula occlusion (`enemyKnown: false`) safely masks `target` / `anti_target`.

### 15. Spawn Exclusion Zones (`SPAWN_SAFE_RADIUS = 600m`), Shield-Routed Collisions & Self-Hit Prevention
- **Guaranteed Safe Spawn Zones**: `generateSector()` in `src/world/sector-generator.js` enforces a strict minimum clearance of `SPAWN_SAFE_RADIUS = 600` meters between both ship spawn points (`SHIP_SPAWN_POINTS`: `(-900, 0)` and `(+900, 0)`) and the outer edge/trigger radius of every procedural entity (asteroids including 1.2x vertex protrusion, debris fields and individual chunks, armed tether mines, gravity wells with `>= 950m` center distance, ion nebulae, and the munitions derelict), with deterministic Y-axis corridor fallbacks if rejection sampling exhausts attempts.
- **Shield-First Capped Collision Damage**: `GameRoom.prototype.applyCollisionDamage(ship, contactPoint, rawDamage, maxCap)` routes all ship-vs-ship (`maxCap = 140`), ship-vs-asteroid (`vClosing * 1.6`, `maxCap = 160`), and ship-vs-debris (`impulse * 0.003`, `maxCap = 25`) impacts through directional shield quadrants (`ship.shields.absorbDamage(...)`) first, applying armor mitigation and preventing mass-scaled raw impulse values from one-shotting ships.
- **`ship.body.id` Shooter Identity**: `createShip()` sets `ship.body.id = team` on the underlying `RigidBody` so projectiles and torpedoes spawned via `weapons.fire*(ship.body, ...)` carry `shooterId: team` and never self-collide with the launching ship on tick 1.

### 16. Drydock-First Root View, Module Bootstrap & Pre-Game Station Tuning
- **Drydock-First Default Route**: `public/index.html` initializes `#lobby-overlay` with `.active` (`display: flex`) and `#btn-open-lobby` with `.active` so visiting the root URL opens directly into Tactical Command & Fleet Outfitting. The top HUD header bar sits at `z-index: 250` (above `.modal-overlay` at `z-index: 200`), allowing players to close the overlay via `GO TO STATION` (`#btn-close-lobby`), station tab buttons, or station slots.
- **Pre-Game Standby Bridge Banner**: While `snapshot.state === 'lobby'`, `#pregame-station-banner` displays readiness telemetry (`PRE-FLIGHT STATION CHECK — UNS HYPERION [HELM] READY`) with direct quick-action buttons to return to drydock (`DRYDOCK / ROLES`) or initiate battle (`LAUNCH MATCH`).
- **Pre-Game Configuration Preservation**: Players can configure their stations in `lobby` prior to battle launch: Helm can engage SAS and Auto-Orient modes, Weapons can set turret aim and target subsystems (with live projectile firing gated until `playing`), and Engineer can rotate conduits, toggle breakers, and solve power flow in real time. Transitioning from `lobby` via `startMatch()` preserves these pre-configured states on matching hull configurations, while `restartMatch()` resets all systems cleanly.
- **Static Import & DOMContentLoaded Race Immunity**: Top-level `await import()` in `public/js/main.js` is eliminated in favor of static ES module imports. Client bootstrapping checks `document.readyState === 'loading'` before attaching listeners to ensure client instantiation never deadlocks on deferred module execution.

### 17. Destructible Asteroids, Strict Station Input Isolation & Turret Articulation Slew / Jam
- **Destructible Asteroids & Dynamic Cover**: Procedurally generated asteroids have finite HP (`maxHp = Math.round(radius * 2.5)`). Kinetic slugs (`railgun`, `torpedo`) chip off cover fragments (1–2 chunks), while total destruction (`hp <= 0`) shatters the asteroid into 4–8 dynamic debris chunks (`dynamicDebrisChunks`, capped at 60) and permanently deactivates physical collisions (`ast.destroyed = true`).
- **Strict Station Input Isolation**: Player bridge inputs are strictly isolated (`helm_input` requires `station === 'helm'`, `weapons_input` requires `station === 'weapons'`, `engineer_input` and breaker/tile/drone actions require `station === 'engineer'`; unseated players with `station: null` are rejected). Vacating a station automatically shuts down continuous laser fire and zeroes continuous helm thrusters in `'playing'`.
- **Articulated Railgun Slew & Mechanical Jam**: The Heavy Railgun traverses at a physical slew rate capped at 20 deg/s (`RAILGUN_MAX_SLEW_RATE = (20 * Math.PI) / 180`). If `railgunHardware.health <= 15`, the turret jams in place (`slewRate = 0`, `railgunJammed = true`), requiring Engineer repair drones to travel to the subsystem node and restore health to unjam. Firing while jammed discharges the slug along the jammed barrel angle (`railgunAngle`).
- **Subsystem Hardware Health Trackers**: `railgunHardware`, `laserHardware`, and `stabilizerHardware` (all initialized to 100 HP) are targeted and damaged via pinpoint railgun penetration (-55 HP), and repaired by Engineer drones when connected to the powered Drone Bay.

### 18. Station Input Isolation, Gunner Free-Look Perturbation & Scissored Multi-Angle PiP Glass
- **Strict Station Input Isolation**: `canHandleInput()` verifies `this.isActive`, that `#lobby-overlay` is not `.active` / `display: flex`, and that no text input or textarea has focus. When switching stations or opening drydock, `syncStationControllers()` immediately calls `setActive(false)` on inactive controllers, clearing active keys, touch stick knobs, and continuous weapon triggers.
- **Gunner 360° Free-Look & Motion Perturbation**: The weapons station decouples viewport viewing angle (`gunnerWorldAngle = ship.angle + gunnerLookOffset`) from the ship heading. Angular velocity (`omega`) and roll disturbances jolt the view via `viewPerturbation`, which exponentially recenters back toward zero at `recenterRate = 2.8 * stabEff` (where `stabEff` scales with View Stabilizer hardware health and power). Damaged stabilizers delay recentering, giving tangible tactile feedback of ship maneuvers on gunnery aim.
- **Scissored Multi-Angle PiPs**: `.pip-slot` elements on the Helm console MUST have `background: transparent !important` and `pointer-events: none` because `SpaceRenderer.prototype.renderMultiAnglePips3D` renders 3D camera angles (dorsal, port profile, stern chase) directly into the main WebGL canvas using viewport scissors matching the DOM bounding rects.

### 19. HUD Non-Overlapping Layout, 3D Camera Banking, and Drone/AI Subsystem Targeting
- **Helm HUD Layout Clearances**: In `#console-helm`, `.rcs-panel` is positioned in the left column (`top: 188px; left: 16px; width: 240px`) directly below `.telemetry-panel` (`top: 16px; left: 16px; width: 240px`), and `.helm-pip-panel` uses a horizontal top-right strip (`flex-direction: row; top: 16px; right: 16px`, max height `90px`) so all 3 PiP insets clear both `.telemetry-panel` and `.helm-minimap-card` (`bottom: 196px; right: 16px`) even on `1280x720`. `.helm-minimap-card` also clears `.virtual-stick-zone.stick-right` (`bottom: 28px; height: 150px` reaching `bottom: 178px`).
- **Camera 3D Roll Banking Sign**: Three.js coordinates place roll around local X. At `angle = 0`, positive `rollAngle` tilts dorsal `+Z` toward `-Y`. Both `updateHelmCamera3D` and `updateWeaponsCamera3D` bank camera `up` with `-sin(roll)` sign (`upX = -sin(heading) * sin(roll)`, `upY = -cos(heading) * sin(roll)`, `upZ = cos(roll)`), so camera tilt matches hull roll.
- **Enemy Edge Arrow Rotation**: `.edge-arrow-icon` is an upward-pointing CSS triangle (`0 rad` points UP / `-Y`). `projectEnemyToViewportEdge` returns `arrowAngleRad` where `0 rad` points RIGHT (`+X`). Rendering applies `rotate(${arrowAngleRad + Math.PI / 2}rad)` so screen-edge arrows align with target direction.

  - Main Drive thrust: $F = F_{\text{base}} \times P_{\text{main}} \times \eta_{\text{thermal}}$

  - Railgun capacitor charge build speed
  - Torpedo tube reloading cooldown recovery
  - Quad-shield regenerative recharge rate

### 24. Shield Regeneration Rate (1%/sec) and < 10% Power Cutoff

### 33. Phase 18 — Body-Frame Angular Kinematics, Manual Rate Governors & SAS Windup Fix
- **Body-Frame Angular Kinematics (`src/physics/rigid-body.js`)**: Converts current Euler angular velocities `(omega, pitchOmega)` into ship body-frame rates `(omegaBody, pitchOmegaBody)` via `omegaWorldYaw = omega * cosP`, `omegaBody = omegaWorldYaw * cosR - pitchOmega * sinR`, `pitchOmegaBody = omegaWorldYaw * sinR + pitchOmega * cosR` (with `cosP = Math.max(0.1736, Math.cos(pitch))`). Torques generated by physical RCS nozzles and the engine gimbal are applied directly to `omegaBody` and `pitchOmegaBody` in the body frame, then mapped back to world Euler rates via `updatedOmegaWorldYaw = omegaBody * cosR + pitchOmegaBody * sinR`, `pitchOmega = -omegaBody * sinR + pitchOmegaBody * cosR`, `omega = updatedOmegaWorldYaw / cosP`. Inverting the ship (`rollAngle = π`) causes `turn: 1.0` in the cockpit to yield negative world `omega` and `pitch: 1.0` to yield negative world `pitchOmega` (matching cockpit screen perspective), while banking 90° (`rollAngle = π/2`) couples pitch into world yaw.
- **Auto-Orient & SAS Body Torque Transform**: Desired world yaw/pitch damping or auto-orientation accelerations `(alphaWorldYaw, pitchAlphaWorld)` are mapped into body frame `alphaDesiredBodyYaw = alphaWorldYaw * cosR - pitchAlphaWorld * sinR` and `alphaDesiredBodyPitch = alphaWorldYaw * sinR + pitchAlphaWorld * cosR` so that SAS and Auto-Orient fire the correct nozzles even when the ship is banked or flying upside down.
- **Manual Angular Rate Governors (`HULL_CLASSES`)**: Defined per hull class (`maxManualYawRate`, `maxManualPitchRate`, `maxManualRollRate`: Corvette `1.6 / 1.3 / 2.2`, Frigate `1.2 / 1.0 / 1.8`, Dreadnought `0.85 / 0.75 / 1.4` rad/s). `getManualRateGovernorFactor` smoothly tapers manual input authority via Hermite curve from 100% to 0% as angular speed in the commanded direction approaches the hull governor limit, while maintaining 100% full authority when braking/reversing opposite to current spin and leaving coasting/collision momentum unclamped.
- **Engine Gimbal & RCS Cooperation**: Eliminates the previous `0.35` minimum RCS floor. `rcsTurnAssist` and `rcsPitchAssist` compute remaining torque needed above gimbal (`Math.max(0, 1.0 - Math.abs(tauGimbal) / tauNominalAct)`), ensuring RCS thrusters seamlessly top up whatever torque the gimbal cannot provide without over-torquing or runaway spin.
- **SAS PID Integral Windup Prevention**: `sasPid.integralError` and `sasPitchPid.integralError` are explicitly reset to `0` whenever manual commands (`manualTurnCommanded` / `manualPitchCommanded`) are active, when angular rates are negligible (`<= 1e-4`), or when SAS is unpowered/disabled.
- **AIHelm Roll-Inversion Compatibility (`src/server/ai-crew.js`)**: `AIHelm` transforms world-space angle errors `(angleDiff, pitchDiff)` into body-frame steering demands `(eBodyYaw, eBodyPitch)` before computing `turn` and `pitch`, ensuring AI bots navigate accurately toward waypoints without spinning out of control after tactical rolls.
